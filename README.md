# 🎮 Dots & Boxes

A modern, responsive implementation of the classic **Dots & Boxes** strategy game. Challenge an AI opponent or play against a friend, choose from multiple board sizes and difficulty levels, unlock achievements, and track your gaming statistics.

## ✨ Features

### 🎯 Multiple Board Sizes

Choose the board size that matches your preferred difficulty and play style:

* **6×6** — Quick and beginner-friendly
* **8×8** — Balanced gameplay
* **10×10** — More strategic depth
* **12×12** — Large and challenging games

### 👥 Game Modes

* **Play vs AI** — Challenge the computer
* **Play vs Friend** — Local two-player multiplayer

### 🤖 AI Difficulty

Three AI difficulty levels are available:

* 🟢 **Easy** — Beginner-friendly AI
* 🟡 **Medium** — More strategic decision-making
* 🔴 **Hard** — Challenging AI designed to test your skills

Track your performance over time with persistent statistics:

* Games played

* Overall performance

### 🏆 Achievements

Earn achievements as you play and improve.

Achievements can be unlocked based on different gameplay milestones and performance objectives.

Customize your experience with:

## 🕹️ How to Play

Dots & Boxes is a turn-based strategy game.

1. Select your preferred **board size**.
2. Choose **Play vs AI** or **Play vs Friend**.
3. If playing against the AI, select a difficulty level.
4. Take turns connecting two adjacent dots.
5. Complete the fourth side of a box to claim it.
6. Completing a box gives you another turn.
7. The player with the most boxes at the end wins.

### 💡 Strategy Tip

Be careful when creating opportunities for your opponent. Sometimes the best move is the one that avoids completing a box and forcing yourself to give your opponent an advantage.
